# 10 How thorough: the four levels and the add-ons

Section 10 of the `scope-case` skill, read to write the card of turn 3 (`SKILL.md` 0.5.4), and again when a result is in or a user who stopped at a level asks to go on; its numbers continue those of `SKILL.md`. The owner asked for "an a la carte menu of refinements based on their desired rigor and token spend, rather than silently assuming them all" (the owner's message of 2026-11-05), and set the levels (2026-11-10): "the levels are: a sketch that just shows the fermis, then a fast that turns them into a monte carlo, then the normal version incorporates review and upgrades a few of the fermis to slightly more complete models. don't forget that the 'full' estimate will do a proper prisma lit search on some inputs." The names Sketch, Fast, Normal and Full are the ones he used in those words (the owner's message of 2026-11-10); the three names of 2026-11-05 (Quick estimate, Draft for review, Full analysis) were the seat's, and no entry of his register records his keeping them when asked.

10.1 The levels. Each runs the seats of `notes/fast_estimate.md` 1.3 named here. Each cost is what the record measured, saying how, until a later run's measured cost replaces it. Every cost is in dollars ("I only care about price, not tokens", 2026-11-08), to two figures, at the list prices of the shared price table (`shared/tools/token_prices.json`): a large mixed total at the ledger's blended $0.59 a million; a small Sonnet helper's total at $4.0 a million, three input tokens to each output token. Promise no time for any level (the owner, 2026-11-10: "I am skeptical that we can predict the time well").

10.1.1 A teaching sheet is a separate track, not a level: one Google Sheet on the owner's Monte Carlo Template that teaches a named reader how the main effects of the policy are estimated, about 100 inputs, each from a logged search of the literature or labelled as judgement, with no documents and no review beyond a check of every input and every formula. When a user asks for a teaching spreadsheet, a worked example in a sheet or a small model to learn from, the turn of `notes/briefs/teaching_sheet.md` 1.2 replaces the card of 10.4, and that item says which turns of this conversation the track keeps.

10.2 The card lists Full. Until its systematic review has finished its first trial (roadmap R4), its line says that review is in its first trial and can follow a Normal draft as add-on 2.

10.3 The add-ons, each offered once the user has a result and not before (recorded as the owner's yes of 2026-11-10; no entry of his register carries it, so the timing is the seat's design), since a long menu up front can overload a chooser unsure of what they want, as a newcomer is (`chernev_2015_choice`, recalled), and a result shows which add-on would pay. They go on one decision card naming those this case would use, each with what it adds and its cost, and the user answers with the letters of those they want. The rows keep the numbers other files cite.

10.4 The card, turn 3. One decision card (2.4): the question, how thorough the work should be; each level in plain words, with what the user receives and its cost on the record, saying in the same sentence when each cost was measured (the Fast level's on one run of 2026-11-16, the Normal level's on the process as it ran before 2026-11-10, the systematic review's on a trial of 2026-11-08 to 11-12); and five options: the sketch; the fast estimate; Normal, with the fast draft shown as soon as it is ready; Normal, shown only once reviewed; and Full, as 10.2 words it. Every option but the fourth shows each result as it comes in, the sketch first. The recommendation is Normal with the fast draft shown, the project's default by the owner's ruling ("I am satisfied enough with this to make it the default, 13% more is fine to give the user something much sooner for them to review and comment on", 2026-11-07), unless the user's opening asked for a quick read. The card names no add-on, and says that more can be added once a result is in. In an exploratory analysis (`SKILL.md` section 1), each level's line also says what research comes first and its cost (10.7). The answer is recorded in `requirements.md`, which `notes/fast_estimate.md` 1.3 reads.

10.4.1 The card says what follows the first draft, the owner's choice between spending on it and stopping with a costed shortlist ("in the initial conversation give the user a choice between 3.8 and 3.7", the owner's message of 2026-11-05): a sketch stops at its estimates; a fast estimate stops after the editor's letter, whose points are the shortlist, the cost of going on being the rows of 10.1 and 10.3; Normal and Full spend on the reviewers' points, the letter of response reporting what was done. This departs from his words: the letter's points are not costed one by one, and the revision follows the reviewers' points, not a ranking of inputs. A user who stopped and asks to go on resumes: after a sketch at `notes/fast_estimate.md` 1.3.1, the author reusing `effects/` and `estimates/`; after a fast estimate at 1.3.4, with the same author. A step that contacts people or sends material to another model is asked first.

10.6 The expert survey is not brought up whichever level is chosen, and is offered as one decision card once a normal draft has finished (`notes/what_a_case_delivers.md`, item 4, with the owner's words, the owner's message of 2026-11-10: "don't bring up the expert elicitations for the fast analysis. leave it as an option for after the medium rigor version"; "whichever level is chosen" is the seat's extension; `notes/fast_estimate.md` 1.3). A user who asks about experts sooner is told that a survey can follow the normal draft.

10.7 The research of an exploratory analysis (`SKILL.md` 1.3) goes breadth first at every level: at Sketch and Fast one researcher writes one short record, about five facts a question at Sketch and twelve at Fast, not yet measured and by estimate at most the cost of one measured record; at Normal and Full, one researcher a question, at the depth the design conversation needs. The one measured run, eight records at a depth set by hand, cost $9.7 to $14 a record, $93 for the eight, which the card states for Normal and Full as measured once.
