The coordinator fills each `[[...]]`, drops an item marked optional where it does not apply, and sends only the text below the line `=== BRIEF ===` as the task. This brief's former items 1, 3 and 5 to 9 are the definition's Method under the same numbers, so that a pointer to `author_fast.md` 3.7 reads Method 3.7; its Method 3.0 to 3.7 are `author_search.md` and its Method 5 is `author_options.md`, under those numbers. What is the same at every dispatch is in the definition, the simplicity paragraph of `notes/briefs/simplicity.md` and the welfare paragraph among it. Where no requirements conversation ran, the coordinator sends, in place of the two sentences of the case paragraph that begin "The requirements conversation has done" and "Check the pool", the sentence "No requirements conversation ran: do your Method 0.1 and 0.2."
