# The form of a factor's distribution

Read this each time you set a factor's low, middle and high, from the fast estimate up (the `fermi-estimator` definition, rule 4). It answers the owner's words of 2026-11-12 (the owner's message of 2026-11-12): "Every time an uncertain value is estimated, the agents should be prompted to think about what the expected distributional form is". Rule numbers below are the `fermi-estimator` definition's. Source keys are in `research/refs.toml`, each stored in `research/sources/<key>/`.

Lognormal is the default, and logit-normal for a chance: name another form only where a row says so. The tool's lognormal is a two-piece lognormal joined at the median, half the mass on each side, each half fitted to one of your ends, so its density steps at the median; this departs from Wallis's two-piece form (`wallis_2014_two_piece`), which joins the halves at the mode. Your high is its 95th percentile, passed one draw in twenty, and the tail runs on beyond it uncapped. The form changes the shape between and beyond your three numbers, never your hand arithmetic (rule 5). At a sketch, leave the form out.

A named distribution. Low, middle and high with a form stay the default for a quick judgement. Where a quantity's shape has a source, such as a fat-tailed event size that a study fits to a power law, or a rate a study reports as a beta from counts, the factor instead names that family: `distribution` and the family's own fields in place of `low`, `middle`, `high` and `form`, exactly as an engine input states them (`cases/README.md`, "An input"; for example `distribution = "power law"`, `min = 1.0`, `alpha = 2.5`), keeping `unit` and `source`. The total tool draws it with the engine's own quantile function on the factor's own random numbers, so a model input of the same name and specification has the same draws; a model input of the same name must state the same specification, or the tool refuses the table. The tool computes its 5th, 50th and 95th percentiles and uses them wherever it uses a low, middle and high (a chain's range, the check of your hand products, Annex A, which labels them computed). A family whose mean is infinite (a power law with alpha at or below 2, a tail index at or below 1) is refused, since no mean of draws estimates it; nothing caps the tail of one that is finite.
